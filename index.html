Settings

⚙️ Settings

Reminders, backup, sync and your Notion connection.

🔔 Reminders

Gentle nudges only, no streak-shaming. Reminders arrive as phone notifications once the app is added to your home screen.

💾 Backup & export

📶 Offline & sync

Offline, every entry saves on this device first and waits. It goes to Notion automatically when you're back online, so nothing is lost.

📖 Bible text

Verses come from API.Bible in CSB, NIV and NLT. Each lookup uses one of your monthly calls, so repeats in the same visit are free.

🧹 Clear my data

Deletes every entry you've saved here, plus your voice recordings. It can't be undone. Copies already sent to Notion are not touched.

🔗 Notion connection

Each entry type pushes to its own Notion database.

Notion isn’t connected yet. Your “Push to Notion” choice on each form is saved with the entry and will be used once it is.
